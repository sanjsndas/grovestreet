    An independent editorial voice on interior design, architecture, and the art of living well — published since 2019.

    Our Story

    Grove Street Architecture began in a small London studio in 2019, founded by three architects and designers who were tired of the glossy, disposable approach that dominated design media. We wanted to create a publication that treated architecture and interiors with the seriousness they deserve — but without the jargon, the elitism, or the endless parade of trend pieces.

    What started as a newsletter shared among friends has grown into an independent journal read by architects, designers, and curious homeowners across the world. We remain proudly independent: no sponsored content, no hidden advertising, no pay-to-play reviews. Every article is written by our editorial team and published because we believe it matters.

    “The most beautiful rooms are those where the materials have been allowed to speak for themselves.” — Elena Marchetti, Editor-in-Chief

    What We Stand For

    Material Honesty

    We believe in celebrating raw, unprocessed materials that age gracefully and tell the story of their making — from concrete to unlacquered brass to untreated timber.

    Independent Voice

    We are not beholden to advertisers, brands, or trends. Our only loyalty is to our readers and to the truth about what makes good design.

    Sustainability First

    Environmental responsibility is not a trend — it is a necessity. We champion low-impact materials, passive design, and adaptive reuse.

    Human-Centred

    Design is ultimately about people. We explore how spaces shape our wellbeing, our relationships, and our daily lives.

    Our Editorial Process

    Every article we publish goes through a rigorous editorial process. We do not accept guest posts or sponsored content. Our writers are paid for their work, and our reviews are never influenced by brands or PR agencies. If we recommend a product or material, it is because we genuinely believe it is worth your attention.

    We fact-check all claims, credit our sources, and correct errors promptly and transparently. If you spot something that needs fixing, please let us know.

    Recognition

    Our work has been featured in design publications across Europe and North America. In 2023, we were shortlisted for the Independent Publishing Award for Best Design Journal. But our proudest achievement is the community of readers who write to us with questions, disagreements, and their own stories of building and living in thoughtful spaces.
