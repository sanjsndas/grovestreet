Before you write

To help us route your message quickly, please note the following:

  • Pitches: Include a short summary (150 words max), your intended angle, and 2–3 relevant writing samples. We publish both emerging and established voices.
  • Reader questions: If you have a specific design dilemma, include a photo or a brief description of the space. We may feature your question in our “Ask the Editor” column.
  • Advertising: We work with a small number of carefully selected partners. Please include your brand, timeline, and budget range.
  • Permissions: For reprints or syndication, please specify the article title and intended use. Allow 3–5 business days for a response.
