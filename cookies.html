1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, to provide a better browsing experience, and to give website owners information about how their site is being used.

Cookies do not typically contain any information that personally identifies a user, but personal information that we store about you may be linked to the information stored in and obtained from cookies. For more information about how we handle your personal data, please see our Privacy Policy.

We also use similar technologies such as web beacons, pixels, and local storage. In this policy, we refer to all of these technologies as "cookies" for simplicity.

2. How we use cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • To make the Site work: Some cookies are essential for the proper functioning of the website, such as remembering your cookie preferences and enabling basic navigation.
  • To improve performance: We use analytics cookies to understand how visitors interact with the Site — which pages are most popular, how long visitors stay, and how they arrived at the Site. This helps us improve our content and user experience.
  • To remember your preferences: We use cookies to remember settings you have chosen, such as your preferred language or display options.
  • To support editorial features: Some cookies help us understand which articles are being read and shared, allowing us to tailor our editorial calendar.

We do not use cookies for targeted advertising, and we do not sell any data collected through cookies to third parties.

3. Types of cookies we use

The table below lists the specific cookies we use on the Site, their purpose, and their duration.

Cookie name Type Purpose Duration
cookie_consent Essential Stores your cookie consent preferences so we don't ask you again on every visit. 1 year
session_id Essential Maintains your session while browsing the Site, enabling basic functionality. Session
_ga Analytics Used by Google Analytics to distinguish unique users and track page views. 2 years
_ga_* Analytics Used by Google Analytics to persist session state and measure usage patterns. 2 years
pref_theme Preference Remembers your display preferences (e.g., light or dark reading mode). 6 months
newsletter_ref Preference Records if you arrived via a newsletter link, to help us measure engagement. 30 days

We only set analytics and preference cookies with your consent. Essential cookies are set automatically because they are strictly necessary for the Site to function.

4. Third-party cookies

In some cases, we use third-party service providers who may also set cookies on your device when you visit our Site. These include:

  • Google Analytics: We use Google Analytics to collect aggregated, anonymized data about how visitors use the Site. Google may set cookies such as _ga and _ga_*. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Email delivery providers: If you subscribe to our newsletter, our email service provider may use tracking pixels to measure open rates and click-through rates. These are only activated when you open our emails.

We do not control third-party cookies. You should check the relevant third party's website for more information about their cookie practices.

5. Managing your cookie preferences

You have several options for managing cookies:

  • Our cookie consent tool: When you first visit the Site, you will be presented with a cookie banner where you can accept or decline non-essential cookies. You can change your preferences at any time by clicking the "Cookie Settings" link in the footer.
  • Browser settings: Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can set your browser to refuse cookies or to alert you when cookies are being sent. However, if you disable cookies, some parts of the Site may not function properly.
  • Opt-out tools: For analytics cookies, you can install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

6. Browser-specific instructions

To manage cookies in your specific browser, please follow the links below:

  • Google Chrome: Clear, enable, and manage cookies in Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox: Enhanced tracking protection in Firefox
  • Safari: Manage cookies and website data in Safari
  • Microsoft Edge: Delete cookies in Microsoft Edge
  • Opera: Security and privacy settings in Opera

7. Do Not Track signals

Some browsers have a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature that sends a signal to websites indicating that you do not want to be tracked. We respect DNT signals. When your browser sends a DNT signal, we will not set analytics or preference cookies. Essential cookies that are strictly necessary for the Site to function will still be set.

Please note that our third-party service providers may not honor DNT signals, as there is currently no common industry standard for how to interpret them.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legislation, or our data practices. When we make material changes, we will notify you by:

  • Posting a prominent notice on the Site.
  • Updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
  • Where appropriate, requesting your renewed consent for non-essential cookies.

We encourage you to review this Cookies Policy periodically to stay informed about how we use cookies.
